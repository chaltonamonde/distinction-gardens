Destination Siaya

Explore Siaya County & Surrounds

Distinction Gardens provides a strategic base for regional discovery, cultural excursions, and travel across Western Kenya.

5 Mins Drive • Approx 2.5 km CONFIRM

Siaya Town Centre

The commercial and administrative heart of Siaya County with local banking, markets, and regional services.

7 Mins Drive • Approx 3.0 km CONFIRM

Siaya Cultural Museum

Discover the rich ethnographic history, traditional Luo musical instruments, and cultural artifacts of Nyanza.

45 Mins Drive • Approx 45 km CONFIRM

Lake Victoria Shores

Visit Usenge and Luanda Kotieno landing beaches for breathtaking freshwater lake sunsets and fishing culture.

60 Mins Drive • Approx 55 km CONFIRM

Kit Mikayi Rock

A towering historical rock tor formation rich with regional legends, climbing trails, and panoramic regional views.
