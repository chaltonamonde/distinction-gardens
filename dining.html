Masterful Preparation

Fresh Catch from Lake Victoria & Charcoal Braai

Our culinary brigade brings culinary passion to life every single day. From pan-crisped whole tilapia seasoned with indigenous coastal herbs to slow-smoked goat ribs on our outdoor charcoal grill, each plate celebrates Kenyan gastronomy.

We collaborate with regional farmers for fresh seasonal greens (sukuma wiki, managu, terere), organic sweet potatoes, and pure local bee honey for our restorative hot Dawa tea.
